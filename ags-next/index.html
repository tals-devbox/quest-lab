Controls and help

Controls, saves, downloads and optional quest hints.

↗

Move & explore

Tap the scene to walk, or use the arrow controls. Press the same direction again or choose Stop to stop. On a PC, start typing to open the command box. Enter submits it; Escape closes it. Press Enter to dismiss messages. Try look and ask about … in the command field.

When the Sheriff asks your name, type it and choose Send. Arrow keys edit text while the command field has focus.

◎

Save & return

Choose Save and wait for confirmation before closing a tab. Use Continue or Load to return. Home keeps this adventure open; new editions with pause support pause while you browse.

Older previews keep their clock running. Export a backup below to keep a separate copy of your progress.

◐

Choose your mode

Exploration playtest turns off unported random encounters while retaining quest rules. Source reference keeps the original encounter boundary, where unported chase or combat can stop the route.

Neither mode claims a complete original campaign. The selected mode stays visible during play and is stored in your save.

⌘

Playing on a phone

Import the private pack in this same browser. Use the command field for the on-screen keyboard and touch controls for walking. Portrait gives the most room for text.

Offline startup needs a complete online visit and an installed pack. Physical Android keyboard, saves and offline checks remain pending. If an older app stays cached, close old Quest Lab tabs and reopen once.

Classic quest hints & recovery

Explore and talk before opening hints. When the Sheriff asks your name, type your name and Send. EGA keeps typed dialogue: try look and ask about …. Click to walk, use the arrow pad or arrow/numpad keys, and Stop (or press the same direction again) to stop. Direction keys do not move the hero while you type.

  1. Meet the healer and learn what she needs.
    Ring hint

    For the tested ring route, choose Fighter and add the 50 creation points to Throwing (60 total). Ask about her ring and prize. Explore the nest outside town; a hero with Throwing can try throwing a rock at the nest. Return the ring to the healer.

  2. Visit the market and prepare for the forest.
    Fruit hint

    Buy apples five times (50 total) from Hilde, then explore north through the forest and the troll approach to Brauggi. Ask about food, and give him the apples. Save some money for food.

  3. Save after one quest. Close and reopen, Load, then finish the other. Revisit both people to check their response.
Recovery commands

Use eat rations, drink healing potion, rest or sleep when appropriate. Supplies are consumed and source restrictions apply; unsafe places may refuse. Type inventory to check what you carry.
