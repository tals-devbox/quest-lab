EGA painted-village playtest

Make a hero, explore Spielburg, and help two people in need. This personal playtest is a small connected adventure, with a painted-anime village. Scenery, props and village NPC colors are updated; the hero and locations outside the village retain reference art.

Use EGA game pack 0.4.3. Choose Exploration playtest at creation for uninterrupted quests. It turns off unported random encounters. Source-reference mode retains the encounter boundary; neither mode is a complete remake.

First time / playing on your phone
  1. Download the EGA 0.4.3 .qlags from the private download section below. GitHub access to the private repository is required; a 404 can mean access is missing.
  2. Return here, choose Import game pack, and select that file. The library shows the installed game version and size. On this development PC, use the local install button instead.
  3. Create a hero and choose a mode. Type commands below the scene; tap the scene to walk. Save and wait for confirmation before leaving.
  4. To update the game, download the new version and import it. The whole pack is downloaded again (size appears beside its link). Old save namespaces are retained; import the matching older pack to recover an older save. Saves do not migrate between builds.

Two-quest guide & optional hints

Explore and talk before opening hints. When the Sheriff asks your name, type your name and Send. EGA keeps typed dialogue: try look and ask about …. Click to walk, use the arrow pad or arrow/numpad keys, and Stop (or press the same direction again) to stop. Direction keys do not move the hero while you type.

  1. Meet the healer and learn what she needs.
    Ring hint

    For the tested ring route, choose Fighter and add the 50 creation points to Throwing (60 total). Ask about her ring and prize. Explore the nest outside town; a hero with Throwing can try throwing a rock at the nest. Return the ring to the healer.

  2. Visit the market and prepare for the forest.
    Fruit hint

    Buy apples five times (50 total) from Hilde, then explore north through the forest and the troll approach to Brauggi. Ask about food, and give him the apples. Save some money for food.

  3. Save after one quest. Close and reopen, Load, then finish the other. Revisit both people to check their response.
Recovery commands

Use eat rations, drink healing potion, rest or sleep when appropriate. Supplies are consumed and source restrictions apply; unsafe places may refuse. Type inventory to check what you carry.

Adventure details & save backups

Selects build, game version, mode, room and recent actions for you to copy. Nothing is sent; no save contents are included.

Restart this adventure with a new hero? Unsaved progress will be lost. Your saved game will be kept, and you can use Load to restore it.

Save confirmation waits for browser persistence. Export a backup to keep progress independently of browser storage. Backups are checked against the exact game build; importing a backup does not alter native game state until you press Load.

Starting a new hero keeps the saved slot. This slice may not implement every original exit or event.
